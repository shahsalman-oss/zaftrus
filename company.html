03 — COMPANY

Built around difficult
problems.

Zaftrus is an Indian deep-tech company working at the intersection of science and engineering.

THE MISSION

Turn difficult science into useful systems.

We are interested in technologies that require real scientific and engineering depth — and in the work required to move them toward something people, institutions and industries can use.

Our operating principle is straightforward: understand the system, engineer the bottleneck, measure reality and iterate.

ECOSYSTEM / INSTITUTIONAL SUPPORT

Build with the people
who move technology.

Zaftrus is developing from within an unusually complete deep-tech support ecosystem—one that combines physical R&D infrastructure, government-backed incubation, industry access and institutional research support.

01

PHYSICAL R&D / OPERATIONAL BASE

NewGen IEDC

NewGen IEDC at the University of Kashmir is the operational home of Zaftrus. Through the incubation ecosystem, we have access to dedicated office and laboratory infrastructure, comprehensive logistical support and professional legal assistance—without the conventional cost of maintaining these capabilities independently.

Office · Laboratory · Legal support · Logistics

02

NATIONAL INCUBATION / JOINT GRANT

STPI × IESA

NewGen IEDC operates as a Spoke Centre within the STPI ecosystem. Zaftrus is a recipient of STPI–IESA joint grant and incubation support, connecting the company to government-backed technology development, mentorship, ecosystem relationships and commercialization pathways.

Grant support · Incubation · Industry network

03

INDUSTRY / DEEP-TECH BRIDGE

IESA

The India Electronics and Semiconductor Association brings the industry dimension to the support ecosystem, opening pathways toward technical mentorship, partnerships, commercialization and interaction with the wider electronics, semiconductor and hardware landscape.

Industry access · Partnerships · Commercialization

04

SPECIFIC CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE

Electropreneur Park 2.0

Electropreneur Park 2.0 is the specific Centre of Excellence under STPI through which Zaftrus is supported. It provides a structured pathway spanning incubation, prototyping, technology development, IP support, mentorship and industry access.

Centre of Excellence · Prototyping · IP support

05

INSTITUTIONAL / RESEARCH FOUNDATION

University of Kashmir

The University of Kashmir provides the institutional foundation through NewGen IEDC and its wider academic environment, connecting Zaftrus with laboratories, researchers, academic expertise, students and entrepreneurship programmes.

Academic environment · Research · Infrastructure

This support is provided through the incubation ecosystem, with no direct payment required from Zaftrus for the office, laboratory, legal and logistical support described above.
