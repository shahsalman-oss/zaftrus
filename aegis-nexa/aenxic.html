AEGIS NEXA / ENABLING MATERIAL PLATFORM FOR AEGIS NEXA

AENXIC
Advanced Electrochemical Nanostructured X-Material Integrated Composite

AENXIC is ZAFTRUS’s proprietary materials research platform for electrochemical water-treatment applications. It is being engineered around the interaction between electrical conductivity, accessible surface area, nanoscale structure and interfacial chemistry.

The objective is to consider transport, interaction, process control and system integration together—without treating any individual component as the entire solution.

AENXIC is being investigated as part of the wider AEGIS NEXA development architecture.

RESEARCH AREAS

  1. Nanostructured conductive matrices
  2. Engineered surface functionality
  3. Electrochemically active interfaces
  4. Transport through hierarchical structures
  5. Fouling and regeneration behaviour
  6. Material stability under repeated operation
  7. Integration with selective separation processes
Development status

This is an active research and development program. Public information intentionally excludes proprietary formulations, fabrication methods, implementation parameters and unpublished performance data.
