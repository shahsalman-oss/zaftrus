01 — TECHNOLOGY

We work where
disciplines converge.

The most interesting engineering problems rarely belong to one field. Our work connects advanced materials, electrochemical systems, computation and system architecture.

THE STACK

A material is useful because of what it enables a system to do.

Advanced materials

We investigate architectures around conductivity, surface area, porosity, transport and selective interaction — properties that become meaningful when engineered into a complete system.

Electrochemical systems

Electrical control can become a powerful engineering variable. We explore how electrochemical processes can be integrated with filtration and water-treatment architectures.

AI and computation

Computation is treated as part of the physical system — from modeling and optimization to intelligent control and engineering automation.
