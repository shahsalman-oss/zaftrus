04 — TEAM

Built by people
who cross disciplines.

Zaftrus brings together academic leadership, deep-tech engineering, software, artificial intelligence and entrepreneurial execution.

The work is deliberately interdisciplinary: physical systems, materials, computation and product development are treated as parts of one engineering problem.

01 / LEADERSHIP

Prof. Naseem
Ahmad Shah

CEO

Prof. Naseem Ahmad Shah is a distinguished academic, scholar, and former Dean of the School of Social Sciences at the University of Kashmir. A Professor of Islamic Studies, he holds postgraduate degrees in Arabic and Islamic Studies from Aligarh Muslim University and a Ph.D. in Islamic Studies from the University of Kashmir, with research focused particularly on Islamic civilization and Central Asian history.

His academic career includes teaching, research supervision, institutional leadership and senior administrative responsibilities at the University of Kashmir, including serving as Professor and Director of the Institute of Islamic Studies and Provost of the University’s hostels. He has represented the University in international academic forums and contributed extensively to scholarship in Islamic civilization, Central Asian history, Arabic and Persian studies, comparative religion and social sciences.

02 / ENGINEERING

Muhammad
Salman Shah

CHIEF ENGINEER

Muhammad Salman Shah is a technologist, researcher and deep-tech entrepreneur serving as Chief Engineer at Zaftrus Technologies. His work spans artificial intelligence, nanotechnology, advanced materials, sustainable engineering and intelligent computational systems, with a particular interest in translating scientific and engineering concepts into practical technologies.

He is the driving technical force behind Zaftrus’s work in next-generation water purification and AI-driven engineering systems, including AEGIS NEXA. His broader interests extend across physics, mathematics, artificial intelligence, computational systems and interdisciplinary scientific research.

03 / COMPUTATION

Suhaib
Mukhtar

CSE HEAD

Suhaib Mukhtar is a software engineer, Python developer and machine-learning practitioner serving as Head of the Computer Science Engineering division at Zaftrus Technologies. He holds a B.Tech in Computer Science from the University of Kashmir with a CGPA of 8.14 and has professional experience developing machine-learning solutions for real-world applications.

His work has included fraud detection, natural-language processing, sentiment analysis, data preprocessing, dimensionality reduction and applied machine-learning problems. He has also worked with IIT Delhi as a Junior Research Fellow and AI/ML researcher on data analysis, slip-detection algorithms, neural-network-based haptic solutions and related research.

04 / AI & INNOVATION

Aasif Rehman
Lone

AI DIVISION HEAD

Aasif Rehman Lone is a B.Tech Electrical Engineering student at the University of Kashmir and an emerging entrepreneur, engineer and innovator. His journey combines engineering education with practical exposure to entrepreneurship, technology and innovation through internships, professional engagements, workshops and startup-oriented programmes.

His interests and projects have spanned electric mobility, tourism technology, artificial intelligence, digital platforms and intelligent systems, including KashmirVista, an AI-driven tourist-safety concept, electric-mobility initiatives and Project JARVIS. At Zaftrus, he contributes to the company’s AI and engineering initiatives with a builder-oriented approach to emerging technologies.
